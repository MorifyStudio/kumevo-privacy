1. Summary

Kumevo is a privacy-first personal budgeting, savings, assets, and cash-flow app. It does not connect to bank accounts or request online-banking credentials. Financial records are stored in the app sandbox on your device by default. We do not sell your financial records for advertising or profiling.

2. Controller

Operator: Morify Studio
Contact: morifystudio@gmail.com

3. Data processed

The app processes financial records and preferences you enter locally, including transactions, balances, budgets, goals, assets, debts, notes, optional receipt images, language, appearance, notifications, and app-lock settings. Face ID data is handled by iOS and is never available to us.

Public market-data requests may be sent over the internet without your personal financial records. If you purchase Pro, the App Store and RevenueCat process purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier to validate access. We never receive your card number.

4. Purposes

Local data is used to provide calculations, budgets, reports, reminders, backups, and security features. Purchase history is used for receipt validation, entitlement access, fraud prevention, and purchase analytics.

5. Storage and retention

Your financial records stay on your device until you delete records, erase app data, or uninstall the app, subject to iOS backup behavior. Exports and backups are created only when you request them and are sent to a destination you choose. Purchase records follow Apple and RevenueCat retention policies.

6. Sharing and processors

Limited processors include Apple for distribution and payments, RevenueCat for purchase validation, iOS for notifications and biometric authentication, and public market-data providers for general rates. Files are shared only through the system share sheet after your action.

7. Security

Kumevo uses the iOS app sandbox, optional biometric app lock, privacy mode, and user-controlled exports. No system is completely secure; keep iOS updated and protect exported files.

8. Children

Kumevo is not directed to children under 13 or the applicable digital-consent age and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

9. Your choices and rights

You can access, edit, export, and delete local records in the app. You may also have rights under applicable privacy law. Contact us for privacy requests concerning data processed by Morify Studio.

10. International processing

Apple, RevenueCat, and public market-data providers may process limited service data outside your country under their own safeguards and policies. Your financial records are not uploaded to a Kumevo backend.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will announce material changes in the app or store listing. Effective date: October 26, 2026.
